# Buzz District — Content Bible

This document is the creative bank for trends, drama, satire, fictional archetypes, and event patterns.

Entries are ideas, not promises to implement all of them.

## Creative rule

Buzz District should feel recognizable without depending on direct copies of real people, real scandals, or real brands.

Prefer:

- fictional brands;
- fictional creators;
- archetypes;
- parody situations;
- exaggerated but plausible urban behavior.

Avoid:

- presenting unverified allegations about real people/companies as facts;
- turning real deaths or serious real-world tragedies into punchlines;
- requiring the player to know one specific real scandal to understand the game.

## Tone

Humor should feel:

- contemporary;
- internet-aware;
- slightly chaotic;
- observational;
- not cruel for the sake of being cruel.

The joke should often be the situation itself.

# 1. FOOD & LIFESTYLE TREND BANK

## Matcha Everything

A sudden matcha wave hits ThreadZ.

Possible effects:

- Cafe demand increases.
- Matcha order probability increases when product system exists.
- Longer prep time creates queue pressure.
- Creator visits become more likely.

ThreadZ examples:

> “Ủa sao nguyên district nay xanh lè vậy?”

> “Quán nào cũng matcha, nhưng quán này xếp hàng thật sự luôn.”

## Blind Box Rush

A fictional collectible shop/product becomes viral.

Effects:

- students/young NPCs flood one district;
- nearby minimarts/cafes gain secondary traffic;
- queues become unusually long.

## Healing Era

NPCs suddenly prefer “healing” activities:

- cafe;
- photobooth;
- coworking;
- parks;
- slow lifestyle services.

Satire target: the way every ordinary activity is rebranded as healing.

## Photobooth Wave

A photobooth becomes the district's mandatory check-in.

Effects:

- group customers;
- creator traffic;
- long session queues.

## Pickleball Craze

Everyone suddenly needs a paddle.

Effects:

- court demand spikes;
- sports retail/minimart traffic increases;
- evening demand becomes extreme.

Potential ThreadZ post:

> “Cả công ty em giờ không hỏi KPI nữa, hỏi sân mấy giờ.”

# 2. WORK & SALARY CULTURE

## Salary Flex

A viral thread asks:

> “25 tuổi lương bao nhiêu là ổn?”

Effects:

- office-worker discussion posts;
- premium business demand temporarily rises;
- humorous comparison messages.

Use fictional numbers and characters.

## Corporate Healing Retreat

An office sends everyone to a “healing” activity that somehow becomes another meeting.

Possible event:

- sudden group demand at cafes/coworking;
- NPC patience lower because everyone is in a hurry.

## Deadline Week

Office workers flood:

- cafe;
- minimart;
- coworking.

Coffee demand high, patience low.

## Remote Work Migration

A popular cafe becomes an unofficial office.

Later gameplay possibility:

- seats remain occupied much longer than service processing time.

# 4. VEHICLE / TRAFFIC / EV CONTENT

## Charging Queue War

An EV charging location becomes overloaded.

Possible interactions:

- long vehicle queues;
- drivers lose patience;
- minimart nearby gains demand;
- charging station upgrade becomes valuable.

ThreadZ:

> “Sạc xe hay xếp hàng mua concert ticket vậy?”

## Charger Hog

One fictional driver occupies a charger much longer than expected.

Gameplay idea:

- temporary capacity reduction;
- queue anger.

## Green Taxi Wave

A fictional electric taxi fleet enters the district at once.

Effects:

- charging demand spikes;
- roadside business traffic increases.

## National Mini EV Craze

A fictional compact EV becomes trendy.

Effects:

- more EV traffic;
- charging service demand.

Do not tie unsafe behavior or accidents to a real automaker.

## Luxury SUV Bro

Archetype:

- expensive SUV;
- impatient;
- dramatic parking behavior;
- exaggerated confidence.

Possible event names:

- “Two-Minute Parking”
- “Just One Quick Stop”
- “Why Is Everyone Honking?”

Use as a fictional archetype rather than naming a real person.

## Road Rage Clip

A fictional traffic argument goes viral.

Effects:

- local Buzz spike;
- creator traffic;
- temporary congestion if traffic simulation exists.

# 6. SOCIAL / CELEBRITY-STYLE DRAMA ARCHETYPES

## Overfriendly Host

A fictional MC/host behaves too casually with guests and triggers debate about personal boundaries.

The game should present this as a fictional social-media discussion, not label a real individual or claim abuse occurred.

Possible effects:

- ThreadZ engagement spikes;
- nearby entertainment venue gets traffic.

## Celebrity School Visit Chaos

A fictional celebrity appearance causes:

- student crowd;
- traffic spike;
- photobooth/cafe demand.

## “Apology Post” Cycle

A fictional creator/business posts an apology.

Then:

- apology goes viral;
- discussion becomes bigger than the original issue;
- Buzz may rise even if reputation falls.

Useful design principle:

**Negative attention can still increase traffic.**

# 10. THREADZ POST PATTERNS

ThreadZ should sound like people casually posting, not system notifications.

Patterns:

### Observation

> “Ủa hôm nay chỗ này đông dữ?”

### Complaint

> “Ai bảo hidden gem vậy, hidden đâu không thấy.”

### Flex

> “Chờ 30 phút nhưng hình đẹp nên thôi.”

### Debate

> “Không hiểu sao cái này hot luôn á.”

### FOMO

> “Mọi người đi hết rồi còn mình chưa đi.”

### Counter-trend

> “Xin phép không chạy theo trend này.”

These patterns can be localized culturally instead of translated literally.

# 11. EVENT DESIGN TEMPLATE

Each event should answer:

## Trigger

Why did the event start?

## Scope

Which business/district/NPC types are affected?

## Simulation effect

What numeric/systemic changes happen?

Examples:

- demand multiplier;
- preference weight;
- patience modifier;
- service-time modifier;
- travel modifier.

## Duration

How long does it last?

## Player signal

How does the player discover it?

Examples:

- ThreadZ;
- visual crowd;
- event card;
- NPC speech bubble.

## Player response

What meaningful action can the player take?

If an event has no gameplay consequence or decision, it is probably only flavor text.

# 13. CONTENT LONGEVITY RULE

Do not build the entire game around one meme.

Content should be layered:

### Evergreen

- traffic;
- queues;
- workplace culture;
- apartment drama;
- consumer trends.

### Seasonal

- holiday behavior;
- weather;
- school periods.

### Fast-moving trends

- viral food;
- creator formats;
- current internet slang.

Fast-moving content should be data-driven and replaceable.

---

# 14. CORE CONTENT PRINCIPLE

Trend content must connect back to simulation.

Bad:

> “MATCHA TREND! + funny text”

Better:

> Matcha trend increases cafe demand, changes order distribution, slows average throughput, increases queues, and creates an upgrade decision.

That interaction is the identity of Buzz District.
